## 2025-07-22 - Background Canvas Optimization with Localized Overdraw & State-Management Bypasses
**Learning:** In a high-frequency animation loop utilizing HTML5 Canvas, using `ctx.save()` and `ctx.restore()` to reset global drawing properties adds substantial context-switching overhead. Overdrawing the entire viewport `(0, 0, W, H)` multiple times for radial gradient overlay rendering degrades frame rate severely. Pre-calculating color components as strings and localizing the bounding box of `fillRect` (specifically for gradients/lights) maximizes performance significantly.
**Action:** Replace `ctx.save()` and `ctx.restore()` with manual cleanups (e.g. `ctx.shadowBlur = 0`). Restrict background clear/fills to the precise bounds of the animated items, and leverage pre-constructed rgba strings for animation.
